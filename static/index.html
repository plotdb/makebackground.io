about this service

Live Background Generator You've Never Seen Before

Have you ever see live backgrounds in some amazing websites or landing pages? Live backgrounds can impress your customers, and bring your design to the next level. But it's not always easy to make, even if you have sophisticated softwares like Adobe After Effect or Blender.

Makebackground.io provides 20 carefully crafted animated backgrounds in both PNG and Webm format for supporting both static wallpaper and motion graphics to fully power your projects with stunning, aesthetic backgrounds, with guideline in mind when we built it:

  • Make It Quick and Beautiful
  • On the Web, on the Fly
  • Easily Customizable for Your Own Style
  • Optimized, with WebGL

To further power up websites with optimized payload size, we also plan to release source codes for all these images with friendly API for web developers. Just Stay tune!



Technical Consideration

To optimize rendering we use WebGL, a widely adopted technology in nowaday browsers. However, some old browsers don't support WebGL such as older version (<=10) of Microsoft Internet Explorer. Performance may also vary across browsers due to their different implementation or their running environment.

For now we only support Webm video generation with Google Chrome browser, but you can still convert webm to mp4 using other tools. We will support more formats (like MP4 or GIF) and browsers for video generation.


Browser Compatibility

Webm video format is currently supported by browsers including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Opera and (partially) Microsoft Edge. Unfortunately Safari doesn't support it.


License

tl;dr - free for both personal and commercial use.

We expect makebackground.io to grow and become sustainable by itself, while it's for now a "proof of concept" and not even a MVP. Thus, all generated images / videos are free for both personal or commercial use. This is subject to change in the future, but you keep the right if you download them before we change the rule.

Additionally, we also plan to open the source code used to generate all these backgrounds; it will, then, be dual license - free for personal use, charge for commercial use. We will make it more clearer once we are ready for it.



Resources

WebGL and Shader language is quite complicated comparing to plain JavaScript, but you may still want to learn more about them. We curated a awesome-webgl list for you to learn more about it, including libraries, tutorials, and people that are also working on creative art.

Animation is always an interesting topic for people who want to make interactive content. We also provide online icon animation with loading.io. It also provides open sourced solution including loading css and transition.css.

We expect to integrate maketext.io for a more sophisticated animation builder, before that you may want to take a look of it.
